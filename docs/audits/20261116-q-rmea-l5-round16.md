## 2. Complete affine-softmax upper bound

For each relation r, its two key/query scores have affine difference a_r x+b_r. Hence the first token weight is sigma(a_r x+b_r). Both utilities u_rt(x) are affine because the complete learned value/decoder stack is linear-affine. Thus

z_r(x) = A_r(x) + B_r(x) sigma(a_r x+b_r),

with affine A_r,B_r. In particular the difference between any two class scores is

f(x)=A(x)+B(x)sigma(a x+b)+C(x)sigma(c x+d),

with affine A,B,C. This remains true with the real shared-parameter constraints; permitting independent A,B,C only enlarges the comparison family.

Multiplying by the strictly positive (1+exp(a x+b))(1+exp(c x+d)) yields an exponential polynomial with at most four distinct real exponents 0,a,c,a+c; each coefficient is affine. Coincident exponents are combined.

Lemma: a nonzero sum of k terms P_i(x)exp(lambda_i x), with distinct real lambda_i and degree(P_i)<=1, has at most 2k-1 real zeros on an interval, counted with multiplicity. Proof by induction: for k=1 this is a linear polynomial times a positive exponential. Divide by exp(lambda_1 x) and differentiate twice. This removes P_1 and preserves the form/nonzero terms of the remaining k-1 exponents (the map P -> P''+2mu P'+mu^2 P is injective on affine polynomials for mu!=0). Rolle's theorem, counting multiplicity, bounds the original zero count by two plus the inductive bound. The identically-zero case is separate and cannot provide strict signed margins.

Consequently a nonidentically-zero pairwise score difference has at most seven zeros, and cannot have prescribed alternating strict signs at nine ordered x values. This is a margin-representability statement, not a statistical learning-rate or task-accuracy claim. Other38 class outputs cannot rescue a strictly correct relative ordering of this pair.

## 3. Constructive original-PQC lower witness

Use theta(x)=pi*tanh(x). In the actual token circuit, set first-layer RY=pi/2 on all three wires, second-layer RY=0, and RZ=theta on all wires in both layers. These are reachable through the original pi*tanh(Linear) parametrization: first-layer RY bias=atanh(1/2); each RZ row selects one scalar feature coordinate; all other rows are zero.

The directed ring M=(CNOT01,CNOT12,CNOT20), acting on a bit string b=(b0,b1,b2), is M b=(b1 XOR b2,b0 XOR b1,b0 XOR b1 XOR b2). Thus M(111)=001 and M²(111)=101. After first-layer RY the eight basis amplitudes are1/sqrt(8). For initial basis b, the two RZ rounds multiply its final basis M²b amplitude by exp(i theta*(|b|+|M b|-3)). In particular amplitudes at000 and101 are exp(-3i theta)/sqrt(8) and exp(i theta)/sqrt(8).

The two relation effects phi+/-=(|000> +/- |101>)/sqrt(2) are also reachable by the original two-layer effect circuit: all angles zero except final-layer RY on wire2 equals +/-pi/2 (preparameter +/-atanh(1/2)). Therefore

q_+(theta)=(1+cos(4theta))/8, q_-(theta)=(1-cos(4theta))/8.

Choose h1(x)=(x,1,0,...), h2=(atanh(1/8),0,...). Token2 then has theta=pi/8 and q_+=q_-=1/8. Set the actual value map/decoders so both selected relations have utilities (1,0); e.g. first value coordinate selects the second feature coordinate and both decoders select that value coordinate. Their biases are zero; all other decoders are zero and biases are-2.

Keeping the original epsilon=1e-6 exactly, write c=1/8. The two class scores are (q_+/-+epsilon)/(q_+/-+c+2epsilon), so their difference has the sign of cos(4theta). Its numerator is (q_+-q_-)(c+epsilon), with a positive denominator. No epsilon deletion or altered attention normalization is used.

Use nine ordered theta/pi values (-.99,-.75,-.5,-.25,0,.25,.5,.75,.99), and x=atanh(theta/pi). The quantum pair has nine alternating strict signed margins, whereas Section2 proves that the full existing affine-softmax head cannot realize that strict sign pattern. A fixed conservative numerical check is margin>.49; it is a fixture tolerance, not a selected task-performance threshold.

## 4. Literature and inference limits

Known quantum Fourier expressivity is explicitly covered by Schuld/Sweke/Meyer, arXiv:2008.08605v2, readpp1–4. Frequency generation itself is not a new result. The exponential-polynomial zero argument is elementary known mathematics. The scoped new question is whether the **combined comparison of the exact normalized output heads including learned values** has a substantive nearest-work distinction; this is unestablished. Earlier QHA raw-input separation and contextual-encoder non-transfer remain intact, not revived.

Yan et al., arXiv:2502.00281v2, readpp1–11, studies unnormalized sigmoid quadratic-gating MoE parameter/expert estimation under its scalar Gaussian regression/least-squares setup and identifiability regimes. Those rates do not transfer to these normalized Born/CE classifiers. The source allows a whole token sequence to be concatenated as one input; within-sequence token independence is not required. A deterministic contextual encoder alone is not a valid objection to all learning theory.

Even a passed fixture establishes only a synthetic complete-head expressivity witness. It does not establish natural-feature support, learnability/optimization, held-out task benefit, general superiority over nonlinear/Fourier classical controls, new mechanism attribution or L5. Existing scopedL4 and failed families stay unchanged. No formal experiment is authorized by this note.
