## Near and far original-source reading

Diedrichsen et al., arXiv2007.02789v5 / DOI10.51628/001c.27664, readpp1,3-6:
cross-products of independent repeated pattern estimates remove squared-distance
self-noise bias, with a variance cost. The source matrix-normal calculation
multiplies the noise-noise variance term by M/(M-1). This is a neuroscience
representational-distance estimator, not a guarantee about our nonlinear
route or different contextual tokens. Independent repeats of the same quantity
and an identified noise model are missing in the target.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.02789v5

Narasimhan et al., ICML2015/PMLR37, readpp1-4: confusion-matrix geometry and
cost-sensitive optimal decisions are known. The metric-gradient characterization
uses explicit regularity and distribution assumptions; its unknown optimal
confusion matrix is not supplied by our development report. Macro F1 should
not be silently treated as a single ratio-of-linear objective or assigned the
paper's concave-objective convergence guarantee.
Source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/narasimhanb15.html
