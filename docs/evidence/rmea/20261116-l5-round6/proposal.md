# Exploratory entity-fusion measurement, not an L5 contribution

The previous candidate-blind adviser found no supported new route in the
aggregate packet. That advice did not inspect this primary proposal. The
proposal below is primary-owned, speculative, and performance-screen-only;
neither novelty nor a new nontrivial theorem is accepted.

## Changed causal link

Original Q-RMEA uses a global rank-one relation effect. The closed ordered
branch applies entity-generated unitaries to that global effect; its formal
seed13 utility failed and will not be rerun. The closed joint branch uses a
six-qubit word/subject/object joint effect and also is not resumed.

Here each entity is instead a mixture of its already prepared token-state
projectors, A=mean_subject rho_t, B=mean_object rho_t. A relation directly
measures each word with the fused mixed effect

  E_r = ((1-kappa_r)A+(1+kappa_r)B+lambda_r*i[A,B])/2,
  kappa_r^2+lambda_r^2 <= 1.

There is no global phi, extra relation unitary or classical-plus-quantum score
residual. The entire effect is a valid quantum density operator and hence an
independent binary effect. Original preparation, epsilon normalization and
linear value/decoder are retained; all40 effects remain independent.
Entity effects are formed after token preparation, not by preparing mean h.

## Direct prior-work collision and exact classical counterpart

Audenaert/Datta/Ozols arXiv1503.04213v3 eqs3.1,4.10-4.17 already define the
partial-swap quantum addition aA+(1-a)B-i*sqrt(a(1-a))*[A,B]. With
a=(1-kappa)/2, the two partial-swap orientations give the boundary
lambda=+/-sqrt(1-kappa^2); mixtures of orientations give the disk above.
The channel, ellipse, positivity and known entropy inequalities are prior
work, not claimed as new. For pure anchors det(2E)=(1-c)*(1-kappa^2-lambda^2*c),
c=|<a|b>|^2, which gives the universal disk boundary by c approaching1.
Mixtures follow by bilinearity. This simple design calculation is not L5 theory.

Fernandes etal arXiv2403.15066v1 already use imaginary cyclic multivariate
traces as basis-independent witnesses; their three-state pairwise-overlap
result must not be generalized to their four-state setting. Conjugation hides
an orientation sign from pairwise squared overlaps for any number of states,
but the old Q global complex effect can already distinguish conjugate tokens.
No old-Q expressivity separation follows from the simple conjugate toy.

Exact classical complex arithmetic computes every fused score. It is a
correspondence check, not a comparator the identical model should outperform.
Quantum hardware speedup/necessity and non-simulability are not claimed.
The source partial-swap EPI concerns density spectra and specified channels;
normalized word weights are not automatically a POVM outcome distribution.
No NLP utility or word-attention entropy bound is imported from that EPI.
